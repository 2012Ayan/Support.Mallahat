Terms & Conditions.

1. Respectful participation

Do not use campaign features to bully, threaten, impersonate, target, or disclose private information about another person.

2. Suggestions

Suggestions should concern school life or the student experience. Abusive, discriminatory, spammy, unlawful, or unrelated submissions may be moderated.

3. Campaign promises

Ideas are proposals, not guarantees. Some changes require school approval, budget, safety review, or other constraints.

4. Fair conduct

Do not interfere with other candidates, school networks, voting systems, or school property. Follow official school election rules.

5. Arcade

Games are recreational and should only be used when permitted by school rules. They must not disrupt lessons or shared devices.
