Privacy & Cookies.

This GitHub Pages version is designed to collect as little information as possible.

Local suggestions

The “Drop an Egg” demo stores submitted text in your browser's localStorage. It is not sent to a server and is not shared between devices.

Cookies & analytics

The starter does not install analytics, advertising trackers, or non-essential cookies. If these are added later, the production site should document the provider, purpose, retention and consent requirements.

Student privacy

Do not collect sensitive student information through the campaign form. A real shared submission system should use a secure backend and follow the school's data-retention and safeguarding requirements.
